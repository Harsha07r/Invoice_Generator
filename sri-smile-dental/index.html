About The Clinic

Dental care designed around your comfort and health.

Sri Smiles Super Speciality Dental Hospital is a dedicated dental healthcare facility serving patients in Tanuku, Andhra Pradesh. Located centrally opposite the Town Hall Complex in Venkatarayapuram, our clinic offers clean, modern treatment suites and a welcoming environment for patients of all ages.

Every visit begins with careful examination and focused attention, ensuring you receive clear guidance and professional dental care tailored to your individual oral health needs.
